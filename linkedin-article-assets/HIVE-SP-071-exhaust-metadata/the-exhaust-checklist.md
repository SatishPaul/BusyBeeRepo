# The Exhaust Checklist: Questions to Ask Your AI Vendor

The content promise ("we do not train on your data") answers the small question.
These answer the one that actually protects your IP.

- Do you train on my content? (the small question everyone asks)
- Who inside your organization can query the metadata of my usage?
- Where is that telemetry stored, and does it ever cross a border?
- How long is it retained, and can I inspect the audit log?
- Does any aggregate of my usage improve a model my competitors also use?
- Can the model run inside my own tenant so the exhaust never leaves it?

The first question protects the message. The rest protect the relationship.

Encrypt the content. Then ask the harder question: who is reading the exhaust.
