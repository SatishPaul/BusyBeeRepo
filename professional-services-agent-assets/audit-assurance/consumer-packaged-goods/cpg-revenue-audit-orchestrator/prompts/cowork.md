# Copilot Cowork (multi-step input assembly)

```
Prepare the input package for the ASC 606 revenue screen for [CLIENT], period [PERIOD].
Step 1: Retrieve the revenue register, the sales-order and proof-of-delivery export, and the trade-promotion accrual schedule from the [SHAREPOINT LIBRARY].
Step 2: Retrieve the customer contracts and the historical return-rate table.
Step 3: Validate that every invoice has an order, a delivery record, and a customer; list any gaps.
Step 4: Produce a single clean input summary (transactions, gross and net amounts, invoice and ship dates) ready for the orchestrator.
Stop and flag if any source is missing or stale. Do not test the revenue.
```
