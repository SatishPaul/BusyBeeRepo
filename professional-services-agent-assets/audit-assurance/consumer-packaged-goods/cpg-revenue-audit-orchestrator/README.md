## What it does

Every audit, a team tests revenue by pulling a sample of forty-odd invoices and tying each one to a shipping document, a contract, and cash. It is slow, it is judgmental, and it looks at a sliver of the population. This pack replaces the sample with a **master orchestrator and five specialist agents** that read the full revenue register, test every transaction against ASC 606, and write plain-English findings to Dataverse. A human judges only the exceptions.

In the bundled Consumer Packaged Goods scenario (Harbor & Vale Foods, FY26 Q4), the agents screened **40 revenue transactions and auto-flagged 3 anomalies**: a period-end channel-stuffing cutoff error, an understated trade-spend accrual, and a returns-reserve shortfall.

## The classification: Orchestrate

Under the agent-first necessity test, this is an **Orchestrate** process. Several specialist agents coordinate the full revenue test, each bounded to one ASC 606 assertion, and hand versioned records to a master orchestrator that stops only at the human approval gate.
