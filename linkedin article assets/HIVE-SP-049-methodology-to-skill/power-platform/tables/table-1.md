## 1 · Methodology Capture Register · Dynamics 365 Project Operations

#### Context
**Persona:** Practice lead. **Problem:** a partner's method lives only in their head and is never captured. **Success criteria:** the method is a structured record of steps, judgment points, and source material.

#### Data model
Create `proserv_methodology`, `proserv_methodstep`, and `proserv_sourcematerial` tables with step, judgment note, input, expected output, and source-link columns.

#### Components
Project Operations knowledge context, a capture-review app, Teams routing, and a Power BI coverage view.

#### Build steps
1. Create tables and relate steps to source material.
2. Capture the method through structured interviews.
3. Record each step and its judgment points.
4. Link source material and examples.
5. Report capture completeness.

#### Demo script
A partner's tacit method becomes a structured, reviewable record. **Wow moment:** the knowledge is finally outside one head.
